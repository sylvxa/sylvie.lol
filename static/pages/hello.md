# What is this?
Hi! This is my blog, where I plan to post about my various projects.

# No, WHAT is this?
This site is made in ***raw*** **HTML/CSS/JS**, with the backend being made in **Python** with **Flask**. The posts are held in a **SQLite** database and rendered in **Markdown**

It is self-hosted on a Raspberry Pi equivalent (I think it's called a Lil Potato from Libre Computers? Not sure.), and it is sent through a Cloudflare Tunnel.

Is it efficient? No. But it is *good enough* considering I'm expecting *at most* 20 visitors a month.

Thank you for reading, and I hope I'll see you again soon!
